# Browcall (GPT Inner Call)

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg?style=for-the-badge" alt="License" />
</p>

**Browcall** lets you connect AI chat tools — like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini — to your own computer, so they can work with your local files and tools through the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

In short: it acts as a bridge between the AI you already use in the browser and the apps, files, and automations, AI-Agent running on your machine.

## What can you do with it?

- **Chat with your local files and tools** directly from ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity in your browser.
- **Automate workflows** by plugging Browcall into tools like n8n, programming language.
- **Use Claude Code** against your own local backend instead of a remote API.
